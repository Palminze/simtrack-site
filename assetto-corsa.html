Head tracking for Assetto Corsa, with the phone you own

Verified with a real head on 12 October 2026 · TrackIR · Windows 10/11

Look into the apex before you turn in, check the mirror, see the car alongside. Assetto Corsa reads SimTrack as TrackIR, so there's nothing to switch on in the game. No TrackIR camera, no webcam, no OpenTrack, and nothing to install on the phone.

Set it up

  1. Start SimTrack on the PC and scan its QR code with your phone. The first time, the setup page walks the phone through a one-time certificate step.
  2. Then start Assetto Corsa. It looks for head tracking when it launches, so start SimTrack first.
  3. In the car, look straight down the road and press CENTER in SimTrack, or the button you bound on your wheel.
  4. Drive. If the view turns too little, raise Turn in SimTrack's View card. Assetto Corsa keeps its own settings, so your other games aren't affected.

Settings that suit racing

Racing looks are small: a few degrees toward the apex. Keep Quick look off and use Turn to set how far the view goes. A small deadzone keeps the view steady on the straights.

What's verified

Turning, nodding and tilting were checked in Assetto Corsa with a real head on 12 October 2026. Leaning (in and out, side to side) is newer and still being tested in games. Assetto Corsa Competizione reads head tracking the same way and is expected to work, but isn't confirmed yet.

If nothing moves

Start SimTrack before Assetto Corsa, or restart the game with SimTrack running. More in help.
