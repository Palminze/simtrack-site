On the test bench

Built, and being tested on my own rig before release. These can still change.

  • Recenter from your wheel. Bind any button on a wheel, button box or controller.
  • Settings per game. The sliders follow the game that is running.
  • Your games. The window lists the supported games on your PC and what each needs switched on.
  • Quick look. Turn past 15° and the view swings onto the mirror. Off by default.
  • "Why is it choppy?" The status line names the cause: slow phone, lost face, or WiFi.
  • Set sensitivity for me. Look at the edges of your monitor and SimTrack sets the sliders.
  • Dark screen on the phone. Tracking carries on behind a black page, so the phone doesn't glare.
  • Delay readout. The window shows an estimate of the head-to-game delay in milliseconds.
  • Session numbers and a card to share. Time tracked, looks to the side, widest view.
  • Stream overlay. A Browser source for OBS that shows where your view is pointing.
  • Mouse look and tilt keys (experimental). For games that don't read TrackIR: the view can drive the mouse, and tilting your head can hold a key.

0.2

  • Leaning: in and out, side to side, up and down, with a Lean slider.
  • Free trial counted in racing days: only days the phone actually tracked.
  • Webcam mode for PCs that have one. No certificate needed.
  • Faster response: frames are processed as they arrive from the camera.
  • Sensitivity for each axis, a deadzone, and recenter from the PC with Scroll Lock.
  • Test Motion: sweep every axis without the phone to check a game.
