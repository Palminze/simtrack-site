Head tracking for Euro Truck Simulator 2, with the phone you own

Verified with a real head on 4 November 2026 · FreeTrack · Windows 10/11

Prop your phone on the monitor and the cab follows your head. Glance at the mirrors, look into the junction, watch the trailer swing on a tight turn. No TrackIR, no webcam, no OpenTrack, and nothing to install on the phone.

Set it up

  1. Start SimTrack on the PC and scan its QR code with your phone. The first time, the setup page walks the phone through a one-time certificate step.
  2. In ETS2: Options → Controls → Head tracking → FreeTrack.
  3. Get in the cab, look straight at the road and press CENTER in SimTrack, or the button you bound on your wheel.
  4. Drive. If the view turns too little, raise Turn in SimTrack's View card. ETS2 keeps its own settings, so your other games aren't affected.

Mirrors without craning your neck

Truck mirrors sit far to the side. Turn on Quick look: past 15° of head turn, the view swings further, onto the mirror, and comes back when you look ahead again.

What's verified

Turning, nodding and tilting were checked in ETS2 with a real head on 4 November 2026. Leaning (in and out, side to side) is newer and still being tested in games. American Truck Simulator uses the same setting and is expected to work, but isn't confirmed yet.

If nothing moves

Check that FreeTrack is selected in ETS2's controls and that SimTrack shows the phone as connected. More in help.
